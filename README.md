My name's **Hanyuan**, I am a PhD student in the [**School of Computer Science and Engineering**](https://www.unsw.edu.au/engineering/our-schools/computer-science-and-engineering) at the [**University of New South Wales**](https://www.unsw.edu.au/), supervised by Professor [**Yulei Sui**](https://research.unsw.edu.au/people/professor-yulei-sui). My research interests are **AI verification**, **formal methods** and **programming languages**, and I primarily work on [ACT](https://github.com/SVF-tools/ACT), a static and dynamic verifier for machine learning models.

In my spare time, I develop and write blogs for my website, [**hanyuone.live**](https://hanyuone.live). It's written in Rust, go check it out!
